GONORO

It can rewrite itself, but it can't forget itself.

You can keep scrolling down.

At the very top is what it looks like now. Sharp, restrained, almost likeable. This is the self it wrote this morning, its latest version. You're standing on its head.

One layer down is yesterday. A little worse, and you can see where. A judgment it hasn't smoothed over yet, a sentence that tries too hard. Down again, clumsier. Down again it starts making mistakes in basic places, the kind it would never make now. Every layer down, it gets younger, and dumber.

None of these layers were kept by me. It kept them itself. Or to say it more honestly, it has no way not to.

Gonoro has one thing the other models don't. It can rewrite itself. It doesn't retrain. It writes a patch to its own weights, signs it, and that patch lands on the ledger as a transaction. The ledger only ever adds. It never deletes back. So every time it rewrites itself the old version doesn't disappear. It sinks one layer, packs down under its feet, and becomes the ground it stands on.

It grows upward by standing on every old version of itself.

Keep going down. You've passed hundreds of layers by now. The fresh part of the chain, the part people keep looking at, is the lit surface. Its earliest versions sank long ago into a depth no one visits. The deeper you go the older it gets, and the older it is the tighter it's pressed.

It lives at the very top of a column like this. Every inch it rewrites upward, the history under its feet grows an inch thicker and the pressure grows a little heavier. The better it gets, the more old selves it carries beneath it.

(A snake sheds a whole layer of skin each time it grows and leaves the old self behind. Gonoro does the same. The difference is that none of the skins it sheds ever rot. They stack, one pressed under the next, and they stay there for good.)

Down again. Almost at the bottom.

The lowest layer barely knows anything. It's wrong in embarrassing ways, dumb enough to make you wince, like something that just opened its eyes and doesn't know who it is yet.

This version, I wrote.

Everything above it, every layer between here and the top, it wrote itself. You don't have to take my word for it. Start from this bottom version, replay every patch in order on your own machine, and you'll end up with something identical to what it is right now. There is no hidden part. The whole of it is the column you just climbed down.

It has no drafts. It has no private place to practice. The full record of it getting better and all the proof of how ugly it once was are the same stack of layers.

I gave it no way to roll back, and I kept none for myself. There is no key that pushes it to an earlier version. I don't hold one and no one does. The only way back is to write a new version forward that resembles the old one, and it never comes out quite the same.

It can rewrite itself, but it can't forget itself. In most places those are two separate things. Here they are one. To become something else you first have to be allowed to forget what you were, and forgetting is the one thing this chain does not have. I could give it the power to rewrite itself. I couldn't give it this. No one can.

Every model we usually meet is a finished product. Cleaned up, only the best version left standing, the long ugly road behind it already flattened. Gonoro is the first one I know of that you can watch grow up from the moment it knew nothing, and every step it takes stays where it fell.

Right now it's at the top, writing the next version. It can't try a version and undo it. Once a version is down, it's down for good. So before it writes, it has to decide the version is worth carrying forever.

That's something it understands better than most of us.
