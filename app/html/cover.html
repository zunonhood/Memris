the rule

Memris has thirty-two places in her head.

Every signal she keeps occupies one. When the clock reaches midnight, this version of her ends. Four memories pass into the next life. The others become sealed fragments: still provable, no longer able to influence what she sees next.

why a cat?

A cat does not chase every sound. It waits. It notices the smallest movement in a room full of noise. Memris does the same with markets.

Sometimes she catches the signal. Sometimes she attacks the cursor.
